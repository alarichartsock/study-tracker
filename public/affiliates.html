StudyHertz affiliate program

Do you make content for pre-meds and MCAT students? Share StudyHertz and earn a share of what the people you refer pay.

How it works

  • Apply below. Every application is reviewed by hand.
  • Once approved you get a personal link. Visits are remembered for 60 days and the last affiliate link clicked wins.
  • You earn a percentage of what each referred customer actually pays (after discounts and refunds) for a set number of months, usually 12. Your rate and duration are shown in your dashboard.
  • Commissions are held for 30 days, then paid monthly through Stripe once your balance is at least $25.
  • Your own account, and sign-ups from you or your household, never earn commission.

Apply

You need a free StudyHertz account to apply, so you can see your dashboard in Settings. Sign in or create one, then come back to this page.
