How it works

Three steps to a steady study habit

  1. 1Set your target

    Add your test date and a daily or weekly hour goal. StudyHertz paces you toward it.

  2. 2Study with the timer

    Pick a section and an activity, start the timer, and focus. Forgot to start it? Log the session by hand.

  3. 3Review and adjust

    See which sections are getting your hours, log full-length scores, and shift time to your weakest area.

Built around the MCAT

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is StudyHertz?

A study tracker built for MCAT prep: a focus timer, study stats by section, a heatmap calendar, goals, practice-exam score tracking and trophies, in one app that runs on your phone and computer.

How does the free trial work?

Every new account gets 30 days with every feature, and no card is needed to start. After the trial you can subscribe to keep adding sessions. If you don't, your account turns read-only and your data stays, ready to view or export.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. StudyHertz works in any modern browser, and you can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app, even offline.

Can I bring my existing study history?

Yes. You can import sessions from a CSV file (including Athenify exports) or restore a StudyHertz JSON export from the Import / Export page.

Is my data private?

Your study data is only used to run the app for you. We don't sell it, and analytics cookies are only set if you accept them. You can export everything or delete your account from Settings at any time.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly and yearly plans can be canceled from Settings, and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Is StudyHertz open source?

Yes. The code is public on GitHub under the GPL-3.0 license.
