Privacy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Your study data

Your account details and the study sessions, subjects, goals and achievements you record are stored on our server and used only to run StudyHertz for you. We don't sell your data. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. If you sign in with Apple, Google, GitHub or Microsoft, we receive your account ID, your name and (if shared) your email address from that provider.

Cookies

Necessary: a session cookie (sid) keeps you signed in, and a short-lived cookie (oa) protects social sign-in. Your theme and preferences are kept in your browser's local storage. These don't need consent and are never shared.

Advertising (optional): Google Ads. If you click Accept on the cookie banner, we load Google's tag (googletagmanager.com, ID AW-18541546430). Google may then set cookies such as _gcl_au and use cookies on Google domains to measure whether our ads lead to sign-ups (conversion tracking) and to personalize ads. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy; see also how Google uses cookies in advertising.

If you click Decline, or never choose, the Google tag is not loaded and your browser makes no requests to Google from StudyHertz.

Changing your choice

You can change your choice at any time: open cookie settings. This link is also in the footer of the home page. If you withdraw consent, the Google tag stops loading from the next page load; to remove cookies Google already set, clear this site's cookies in your browser. You can also manage ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/alarichartsock/study-tracker or use "Report a bug" in the app.
